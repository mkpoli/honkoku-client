# Direction C — 遊戯

Playful and rewarding without becoming a toy. A bright, sophisticated palette on off-white: persimmon 柿, matcha green, ultramarine, a warm yellow, each with a role (project category, status, reward, warning). Rounded cards (12px), bold rounded sans (M PLUS Rounded or Zen Maru Gothic with Inter), large friendly numerals. Progress is rings and stacked bars; the workbench keeps a small XP bar and a streak counter with a subtle celebration state for the page just completed. Rewards are visible but the editor itself stays calm and legible, with a serif for the transcription. Dark theme is a deep navy with the same saturated accents, softened.
Output directory: design/explorations/c/
