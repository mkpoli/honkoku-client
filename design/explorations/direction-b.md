# Direction B — 工房

A professional tool, dark first. Charcoal surfaces with a slight blue-grey cast, high-contrast near-white text, dense panels with 1px borders, a command palette (⌘K) hinted at in the title bar, a status bar along the bottom with save state, page position, points and streak in small monospace figures. Type: a humanist sans (Inter or IBM Plex Sans with Noto Sans JP), monospace for identifiers and counts, and a serif only inside the transcription editor. One accent, amber or teal, used sparingly for the active column, focus rings and primary actions. Radius 4px. Light theme is the same structure on cool light grey. Everything keyboard-reachable; shortcuts are visible on hover labels.
Output directory: design/explorations/b/
