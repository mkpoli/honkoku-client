F 藍 uses warm white surfaces for browsing in light mode and deep indigo with slate panels in dark mode. Persimmon marks selection and work in progress. Restrained gold identifies completed pages and leading contributors. Manuscript images retain their warm paper colours in both themes.

Light tokens: background #F4F2EE, surface #FFFFFF, text #202632, secondary text #68717D, border #DDDCD6, accent #D95936, selected surface #FBEDE7, gold #AE8842, progress track #E3E5E7, connected indicator #3E9150.

Dark tokens: background #131A29, surface #1D293A, inset surface #182234, toolbar #243247, inactive control #28364B, text #F0EEE8, secondary text #B4BECD, border #354359, accent #D95936, accent text #ED8968, selected surface #382D33, gold #C1A16A. Page status pairs grey, persimmon and gold with the labels 未着手, 翻刻中 and 完了.

Noto Sans JP is used for navigation, document titles, metadata and controls. Noto Serif JP is reserved for the vertical transcription. Target sizes are 16–18 px for controls and metadata, 20–22 px for section headings, 28–32 px for screen titles and 27–30 px for transcription. Numerals use tabular spacing. Furigana and the two lines of 割書 use approximately half the transcription size.

Panels have 8 px corners; controls have 6 px corners. Borders are 1 px. Spacing follows 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 px steps, with 16 px pane gaps. Surfaces have no elevation shadows.

The home themes share the same three-column structure. Project navigation pushes the collection and entry panes into place while retaining the activity column. In the workbench, the editor is left of the original; the side-swap control remains visible. Matching 左丁 and 右丁 dividers connect the editor to the spread. The page strip is shown expanded. Rare insertion marks use readable Japanese button names. Rankings and personal records appear on home, with no gamification in the workbench.
