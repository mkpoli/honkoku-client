# Direction D — 編集室

Editorial and typographic, like a well-set Japanese newspaper or magazine. Near-monochrome: white paper, black text, cool grey rules, one accent red used for the active state and for counts that matter. Strong hierarchy through size and weight in a gothic sans (Noto Sans JP with Inter), tabular numerals, visible grid lines, tables rather than cards for lists, big figures for progress. Radius 0. Dark theme is warm dark grey with the same red. The workbench feels like a typesetting desk: the facsimile in a hairline frame, the editor with ruled columns, the palette as a compact keyboard-like grid of glyphs.
Output directory: design/explorations/d/
