# Direction A — 紙と墨

A scholar's reading room. Warm paper surfaces (off-white with a faint cream), ink-black text, serif Japanese (明朝: Noto Serif JP or Shippori Mincho) for content and headings, a quiet sans for controls. Vertical text is the centre of gravity. Accent colours come from the manuscript tradition: 朱 vermilion for marks, corrections and the active column; 藍 indigo for links and selection. Rules and hairlines instead of boxes and shadows; generous margins; 2px radius. Dark theme is a lamp-lit desk: deep brown-black paper, warm off-white ink, the same vermilion and a lifted indigo. Progress is shown as thin ink bars and figures, never as coloured chips.
